Welcome! I'm Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab at the National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center [(NERSC)](https://www.nersc.gov/). I'm interested in the intersection between Machine Learning and the Natural Sciences. More specifically, what can we learn about nature by leveraging modern algorithmic development and develop new algorithms powered by scientific knowledge. These include anomaly detection of new physics processes, detector deconvolution/unfolding, and large surrogate models for detector simulation.

As a physicist by trade, my [PhD Thesis](https://cds.cern.ch/record/2781479/files/CERN-THESIS-2021-138.pdf) involded the development and application of new machine learning methods to data collected by the CMS Experiment at the Large Hadron Collider, exploring particle collisions with messy signatures, such as all-hadronic final states.
