Modern day collider experiments have amassed billions of particle collisions used to understand particle interactions. Due to the complex nature and rarity of some physics interactions, modern data analysis tools have become an important pillar for scientific research. In this talk I will highlight the unique aspects of data analysis in collider physics and how the combination of modern machine learning methods with decades of experimental and theoretical innovation are combined to accelerate scientific discovery.
